Privacy Policy

Effective 9 July 2026 · Publisher: prodavam-nemovitost.cz, s.r.o. · info@prodavam-nemovitost.cz

The short version: everything stays on your device. RosterMonitor runs no servers, contains no analytics or advertising, and never sends your data to us or to anyone else. We — the publisher — have no technical way to see your schedule, your credentials, or anything else the app works with.

1. Who we are

RosterMonitor (“the app”) is published by prodavam-nemovitost.cz, s.r.o., registered office Rybná 716/24, Staré Město, 110 00 Prague 1, Czech Republic, Company ID (IČO) 21964718, File C 408978 kept by the Municipal Court in Prague (“we”, “us”). For anything related to privacy, contact us at info@prodavam-nemovitost.cz.

2. What the app stores — locally, on your device only

  • Crew-portal credentials. The login you enter to connect the app to your crew-roster web portal. Stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Roster and duty data. Your schedule, downloaded by the app directly from that portal. Stored in a local database on your device.
  • Computed results and preferences. Limit calculations, alerts and settings derived from the above. Stored locally.

None of this data is transmitted to us or to any third party by the app.

3. What we collect: nothing

  • The app contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party data-collecting SDKs.
  • We operate no servers. The app has no backend of ours to talk to.
  • The app’s only network connections go directly from your device to (a) the crew-portal server you yourself configure, using your own credentials, and (b) Apple, for subscription processing. We are not a party to either connection and cannot see their contents.

The app may also contain links (for example to public aircraft-tracking websites) that open outside the app, in your browser; those sites’ own privacy policies apply.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple account. We never receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Face ID / Touch ID

The optional app lock uses the biometric authentication built into iOS. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and is never accessible to the app or to us.

6. Notifications and alarms

All notifications and wake-up alarms are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification server of ours is involved.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings offers a full data deletion that erases the local database and also removes saved credentials from the iOS Keychain.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local database and settings. Credentials saved in the iOS Keychain may, however, persist after uninstallation — run the in-app data deletion first to remove everything, including the Keychain entries.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

All processing happens on your device, under your sole control; we never receive any of your personal data from the app, so there is nothing held by us to access, correct, export or erase. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example from support e-mail correspondence), you can exercise your GDPR rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection — by contacting info@prodavam-nemovitost.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

9. Children

The app is intended for professional flight crew and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its effective date, is always published at this address.
